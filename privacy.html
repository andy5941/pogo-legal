Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 26, 2026

POGO: Vertical Jump Trainer ("POGO," "we," "our") respects your privacy. This policy describes what data we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & profile: When you sign in (e.g. with Apple), we receive an identifier and, if you allow it, your name. We store your profile (name, height, weight, standing reach, vertical, goals, training preferences, and optional details like birth date) in our database.
  • Training data: Workout logs, vertical and weight measurements, 40-yard and broad jump times, program progress, custom workouts, and any content you enter in the app.
  • Subscription status: We receive and store whether you have an active POGO Pro or Coach Pro subscription (via our payment provider) to unlock features.
  • Coaching data (Coach Pro): If you use coaching features, we store team names, roster information (including athletes you add or invite), metrics you record for athletes (e.g. vertical, 40-time, weight), attendance, and optional coach display names. Athletes you coach can see that they are linked to you; they do not see other athletes on your roster.
  • AI features (optional): If you use in-app AI (e.g. chat or workout generation), we may send the content you submit to our AI provider to deliver those features. We do not use that content to train general-purpose models.

We do not sell your personal information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Provide and personalize the app (program, workouts, progress tracking).
  • Process and manage your POGO Pro or Coach Pro subscription.
  • Provide coaching features (teams, rosters, metrics, invites) when you use Coach Pro.
  • Deliver AI-powered features when you use them.
  • Improve the app and fix issues.
  • Comply with law and protect our rights.

3. Who We Share Data With

  • Supabase: Our database and authentication provider (hosted infrastructure). They process data on our behalf under their privacy terms.
  • Apple: For Sign in with Apple and, if you subscribe, for in-app purchase processing. Subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat: For subscription management and entitlement checks. Subject to RevenueCat’s privacy policy.
  • AI provider: If you use AI features (e.g. chat or workout generation), we send the relevant prompts and context to our AI service provider to generate responses. Their use of data is governed by their terms and our agreement with them.

We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can request account and data deletion by contacting us (see Contact below). We will delete or anonymize your data in line with applicable law.

5. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to: access and receive a copy of your data; correct or update your data; request deletion of your data; object to or restrict certain processing; withdraw consent where we rely on it. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures (including encryption and secure providers) to protect your data. No system is 100% secure; we will notify you of significant breaches where required by law.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe we have done so, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version in the app or at the same URL and update the "Last updated" date. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
